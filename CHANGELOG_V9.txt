BaldiAnimStudio V9
- Smooth interpolation rewritten as frame-spacing-aware cubic Hermite.
- Interior tangents are calculated in value/frame units, so uneven key spacing no longer creates fake slowdowns at each key.
- Smooth endpoints use the real adjacent slope instead of easing to zero.
- Linear remains exact straight interpolation.
- Step remains a true hold with no transition until the next key.
- Playback preview now evaluates fractional frames at requestAnimationFrame rate instead of flooring every preview sample to an integer frame.
  This removes the repeated-frame/stutter feeling in the editor while keeping keyframes frame-based.
